## Project: Two-Level MC

1. Get "familiar" with **NGSolve**.  

    a) Discretizing the following model PDE
        $$
        -\frac{\partial}{\partial x} \left(\kappa_1 (x,y) \frac{\partial}{\partial x} u\right) - \frac{\partial}{\partial y} \kappa_2 (x,y) \frac{\partial}{\partial y} u + \sigma^2 u = f(x,y),\\\\
        \qquad (x,y)\in \Omega = (0,1)^2\subset \mathbb{R}^2.
        $$
        on an uniform triangular mesh with mesh size  

---

### Example: $f(x,y) = 0$, $\sigma^2 = \text{const} = 0.25$.

$$
\Omega = [0, 1] \times [0, 1] \\
\qquad \text{with } u |_{x=0} = \text{ given }=1 \\
\qquad \text{and  } u |_{\partial \Omega \\ \left\{x=0\right\}} = 0
$$

$$
\begin{cases}
    \kappa_1 = \kappa(x,y) = \begin{cases} 1 + x^2 + y^2 \text{ for test purposes}\\
        \text{ otherwise given as a function}
        \end{cases}\\ 
        \qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\begin{cases}
        (x,y)\to \text{ from the mesh }\\
        \text{returns } a(x,y).
        \end{cases}\\
    \kappa_2 = \kappa_1 
    \end{cases}
$$

$\to$ End up with a matrix $A$ in sparse format (from NGSolve)  

$\to$ a linear problem $A\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{f}$  

$$
\overline{A} = \begin{bmatrix} A & * \\
                               * & A_{\text{boundary}} 
               \end{bmatrix}
$$
